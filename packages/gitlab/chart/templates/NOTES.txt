GitLab 19 no longer includes PostgreSQL, Redis, or MinIO. This chart does not
deploy replacements or migrate their data. Configure externally managed
PostgreSQL 17, a supported Redis or Valkey release, and GitLab-supported
external object storage before installing or upgrading GitLab.

Existing users of bundled services must complete the staged migration on GitLab
18.11 before upgrading to chart 10. See docs/gitlab-19-upgrade.md.
